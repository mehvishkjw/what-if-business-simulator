How the simulator works

  1. Demand = today's 10,000 units/month × (1 + growth target), adjusted by marketing lift and price elasticity.
  2. Four gates each have a capacity: Workforce (people × output, + overtime), Inventory (supplier inflow + safety stock), Production (lines × shifts, + outsourcing), Logistics (trucks × loads, + 3PL).
  3. Units sold = the smallest of demand and every gate. That smallest gate is the bottleneck.
  4. New hires ramp up over 3 months; demand ramps over the rollout months, so the 12-month chart shows the transition.
  5. Profit = revenue − variable cost − added labour, shifts, overtime, trucks, outsourcing premium and marketing.
